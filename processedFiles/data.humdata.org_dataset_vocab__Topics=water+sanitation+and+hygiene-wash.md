- [Ethiopia - Rivers](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/ethiopia-rivers-icpac)





[3iS](https://data.humdata.org/organization/3is?sort=metadata_modified+desc)



700+ Downloads







Time period of the dataset: 14 August 2017 - 14 August 2017


















The dataset "Ethiopia - Rivers" represents the river lines of Ethiopia, sourced from DIVA-GIS. It provides vector data indicating the locations of rivers across the country. The dataset falls under the Inland Waters category, which includes features related to drainage systems, water utilization plans, and hydrological characteristics. The data is available for public access. The dataset was published on August 14, 2017, and it can be downloaded or viewed through the ICPAC Geoportal.

- [Sudan - Multi Sectoral Needs Assessment - 2025](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/unhcr-sdn-2025-msna-v2-1)





[UNHCR - The UN Refugee Agency](https://data.humdata.org/organization/unhcr?sort=metadata_modified+desc)











Time period of the dataset: 01 July 2025 - 01 September 2025


















The Multi-Sector Needs Assessment (MSNA) was conducted between July and September 2025 to identify the priority needs of refugees and asylum-seekers in Sudan and to inform planning for the 2026 Country Refugee Response Plan (CRP).

UNHCR implemented the assessment across 12 states, covering more than 3,000 households and approximately 14,000 individuals. The findings contribute to both the Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan (HNRP) and the Country Refugee Response Plan, supporting a more targeted, coordinated, and evidence-based humanitarian response.

- [Lebanon - Vulnerability Assessment of Syrian Refugees in Lebanon, 2025](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/unhcr-lbn-2025-vasyr-v2-1)





[UNHCR - The UN Refugee Agency](https://data.humdata.org/organization/unhcr?sort=metadata_modified+desc)











Time period of the dataset: 15 May 2025 - 28 June 2025


















After more than ten years into the Syria conflict, Lebanon has found itself at the forefront of one of the worst humanitarian crises. The economic downturn, steep inflation, and other challenges have pushed vulnerable communities in Lebanon - including Syrian refugees - to the brink, with thousands of families sinking further into poverty.
The 2025 Vulnerability Assessment of Syrian Refugees in Lebanon (VASyR) was the thirteenth annual survey assessing the situation of Syrian refugees in Lebanon to identify changes and trends in their vulnerabilities. Considering the prolonged socioeconomic crisis in Lebanon, it was crucial to provide needs-based estimates on Syrian refugees in the country.
At the time of the survey, the Syrian refugee population in Lebanon was one of the largest concentrations of refugees per capita in the world.

- [Whole of Afghanistan Assessment 2025 (Analysis) \[By Request Only\]](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/whole-of-afghanistan-assessment-2025-analysis)





[REACH Initiative](https://data.humdata.org/organization/reach-initiative?sort=metadata_modified+desc)











Time period of the dataset: 13 July 2025 - 24 August 2025


















The Whole of Afghanistan Assessment (WoAA) 2025 is a multi-sectoral needs assessment conducted through in-person, household-level interviews across all provinces and districts of Afghanistan. The dataset provides a comprehensive analysis of households’ sectoral and intersectoral needs across various domains, including health, education, nutrition, WASH, food security, and protection, among others.
The results are statistically representative of male-headed households at the district level, and of female-headed households in nine provinces. In addition, the assessment includes displaced population groups, such as internally displaced persons (IDPs) — both those displaced recently (2025) and those displaced non-recently (2023–2024) — as well as cross-border returnees, classified similarly as recent and non-recent based on their displacement timeframe. These displaced population groups are represented at the regional level.

Humanitarian Situation Monitoring aims to collect and then triangulate information regarding service provision, sectoral needs, and vulnerabilities in Afghan communities, in order to then support geographical and sectoral prioritizations within the 2022 humanitarian response in Afghanistan, particularly in light of the rapidly evolving context in Afghanistan. The key-informant-based HSM methodology uses settlements as the unit of analysis, using a structured survey tool to interview key informants about the situation in their settlement. A sampling frame covering all 401 districts in Afghanistan was used, covering a minimum of 10% of total settlements in each district. To achieve geographical spread across each district, at least three key informant interviews (KIIs), in a number proportionate to the number of settlements, were conducted in each Basic Service Unit (BSU). To determine a BSU (defined as an economic/geographic service unit which relies on the same services, i.e. healthcare clinics and schools, and common public spaces, i.e. markets and roads), participatory mapping of settlements and services available was conducted prior to data collection. Any analysis conducted using the data in this file will generate statistically non-representative results, as the key informant methodology is indicative
